Finite-Time Corrections to Landauer Erasure in Overdamped Potentials

Dr. Clara Moreau
Ecole Normale Superieure, Paris
Abstract

Using optimal transport on Wasserstein-2 manifolds, we compute the exact minimum dissipated heat required to reset a symmetric double-well bit in finite duration tau.

1. The Thermodynamic Cost of Bit Erasure

Erasure of a single symmetric memory bit reduces Shannon entropy by ΔS=−kBln⁡2\Delta S = -k_B \ln 2ΔS=−kB​ln2. In the quasistatic limit τ→∞\tau \to \inftyτ→∞, the average work obeys Landauer's principle:

⟨W⟩≥kBTln⁡2\langle W \rangle \ge k_B T \ln 2⟨W⟩≥kB​Tln2

2. Finite-Time Optimal Transport Bound

For an overdamped Brownian particle with mobility μ\muμ evolving from probability density ρ0\rho_0ρ0​ to ρτ\rho_\tauρτ​ over finite duration τ\tauτ, the minimum excess dissipation is governed by the squared L2L^2L2-Wasserstein distance W2(ρ0,ρτ)2\mathcal{W}_2(\rho_0, \rho_\tau)^2W2​(ρ0​,ρτ​)2:

⟨Wdiss⟩min⁡=W2(ρ0,ρτ)2μτ\langle W_{\text{diss}} \rangle_{\min} = \frac{\mathcal{W}_2(\rho_0, \rho_\tau)^2}{\mu \tau}⟨Wdiss​⟩min​=μτW2​(ρ0​,ρτ​)2​

Combining both regimes gives the tight finite-time bound ⟨W⟩≥kBTln⁡2+W22μτ\langle W \rangle \ge k_B T \ln 2 + \frac{\mathcal{W}_2^2}{\mu \tau}⟨W⟩≥kB​Tln2+μτW22​​, verified experimentally in feedback optical tweezers.
